DMCA / Copyright Policy

WallFlixHub respects the intellectual property rights of creators, artists, photographers, and copyright owners.

If you believe that any wallpaper or content available on WallFlixHub infringes your copyright, you can request removal by sending a DMCA / copyright notice.

How to Submit a DMCA Request

Please include the following information in your email:

  • Your full name or company name.
  • Your contact email address.
  • The exact URL of the wallpaper or content you want removed.
  • Proof that you own the copyright or are authorized to act for the owner.
  • A statement that you believe the use of the content is not authorized by the copyright owner, agent, or law.
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate.
